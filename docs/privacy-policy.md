# Shift Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Shift is an email app for Gmail made by [TALKK / legal name] ("we"). This policy explains what Shift does with your information. Short version: **your email stays on your device. We don't have servers that receive it.**

## What Shift accesses

When you sign in with Google, you allow Shift to use the Gmail API with the `gmail.modify` permission. Shift uses it only to provide the email features you use:

- read your messages, threads and labels so they appear in the app;
- send email, replies and forwards you write;
- create, update, send and delete your drafts;
- archive, label, star, mark read or unread, mark as spam and move messages to Trash when you ask.

Shift does not permanently delete email.

## Where your data goes

- **Your email and contacts stay on your device.** Shift talks to Google's servers directly from your device and stores a copy of your mail in a local database on that device so the app is fast and works offline.
- **Your Google sign-in** is stored in your device's secure keychain (on a Mac, the macOS Keychain).
- **We don't receive, store, sell or share your Google data**, and we don't use it for advertising, profiling, or to train AI models.
- Shift loads small sender logos from Google's public favicon service using the sender's web domain (for example `stripe.com`), not your email content.
- If you add your own AI provider API key in Settings, the key is stored in your device's keychain. [Update this section before releasing any AI feature: say what content is sent to the AI provider you chose and when.]

## Google API Services User Data Policy

Shift's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing your data

- **Settings → Accounts → [account] → Log out** removes that account's sign-in and all of its mail stored by Shift from your device.
- Deleting the app removes Shift's local data.
- You can also revoke Shift's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Signing out of Shift does not delete anything in your Gmail account.

## Children

Shift is not directed at children under 13.
