title: Label chips and quicker actions
---

A tidier email list and a few fixes.

- **Labels show in the email list** as small chips in their colour. Labels without a colour show a grey dot.
- **Hover an email** to Reply, Archive or Delete it.
- **Replies fold the previous message** behind a ••• button, like in conversations. It's still sent in full.
- **Clean up Inbox can be minimised** while it's sorting.
- **Restarting after an update reopens Shift on its Dock icon** instead of adding a second one. This applies from the next update onwards.
- **Forwarded attachments respect the 25 MB limit**, with a message for files that don't fit.
- **Auto-label and Clean up make better calls** on borderline emails, using medium effort with Claude.
- **Smaller fixes**: tighter email cards in the list, the "Write your message…" hint in replies without a signature, and a centred + next to Labels.
