title: Pins, drafts and cleaner deletes
---

A round of fixes to pins, drafts, deleting and notices.

- **Pinned at a glance.** The pin button turns solid when an email is pinned, and the plain Inbox list keeps pinned mail on top.
- **Deleting sticks.** Deleting from Spam no longer brings the email back, and Delete inside Trash now deletes for good, after asking.
- **Faster drafts.** Drafts open instantly, a discarded draft leaves the list straight away, and clicking outside the composer keeps your draft with a card to reopen it.
- **Friendlier notices.** Messages at the bottom of the window are a light card with an icon.
- **Settings stay put.** Fixed the sidebar order sometimes resetting.
