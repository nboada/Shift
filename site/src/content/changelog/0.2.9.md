title: Snappier, steadier
---

Moving through your mail feels quicker, menus and messages look more polished, and Shift keeps your changes safe when you quit.

- **Faster next email.** After you archive, delete or move an email, the next one opens straight away: Shift loads the emails around the one you're reading ahead of time.
- **Avatars appear at once.** Sender logos and initials no longer flash empty when you switch mailboxes.
- **Smoother menus.** Menus grow out of the button that opened them, and tooltips stay out of the way while one is open.
- **New notifications.** Messages at the bottom of the window have a close button, pause while you hover them, and slide in gently.
- **Tidier toolbar.** Delete comes first, then Archive. Hover buttons on list rows sit in a rounded container.
- **Remembers your window.** Shift opens at the size and position you left it.
- **No more stray Reload menu.** Right-clicking empty space no longer offers Reload or Inspect Element; selected text still gets Copy and Look Up.
- **Changes survive quitting.** An archive, move or label change made just before quitting is no longer lost; it's sent the next time Shift syncs.
