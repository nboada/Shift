---

Smaller improvements and fixes after 0.2.0.

- **Clean up Inbox works in the background.** Close it and keep working; a small card in the corner tells you when your Inbox is sorted. Each group now opens and closes on its own.
- **Check for Updates…** in the Shift menu.
- **Choose which account new emails come from** in Settings → Composer.
- **Create labels from the sidebar** with the + next to Labels. With all accounts showing, the label is made in each of them.
- **The test notification** now shows even while Shift is in front.
- **Open at login** is listed as Shift in macOS, not under the developer's name.
- A new AI icon, and tooltips that show above open windows.
