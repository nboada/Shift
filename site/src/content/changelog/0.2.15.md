title: Labels you can see, follow-ups that follow
---

Labels are visible everywhere now, and replies can move a conversation between your own labels.

- **Labels on every email.** Each email shows its labels at the start of the subject, including on that label's own page, and the open email lists its labels under the subject.
- **Follow-ups.** In Settings → Inbox, pick your "needs my reply" and "waiting for a reply" labels. When you reply, the conversation moves to "waiting", and back when they write again, even for replies sent from Gmail on the web or your phone.
