title: AI with your own account, and faster triage
---

AI features that run on your own AI provider account, plus quicker ways to work through a busy inbox.

- **AI with your own account**: Write with AI and Draft a reply in the composer, Auto-label for new mail, and Clean up Inbox, which sorts newsletters, notifications and finished conversations for you to archive. Bring an Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI, Mistral, OpenRouter or local Ollama account, pick a model per task, and set your own writing rules.
- **Select several emails** with ⌘-click or ⇧-click, or all of them with ⌘A, then archive, delete, pin or mark them in one go. ⌘Z undoes the lot.
- **Replies open in a window** like a new email. Prefer them under the email? Switch it in Settings → Composer.
- **Quoted history is folded** behind a small ••• button, so each reply shows its own text.
- **Escape in the composer** asks whether to save the draft or discard it; press it twice to discard.
- **A short walkthrough** on first launch, and a cleaner look throughout.
