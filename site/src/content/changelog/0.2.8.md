title: Tidier Move to menu
---

Move to now lists only folders, and Shift is sturdier when an AI reply comes back malformed.

- **Move to lists folders, not labels.** It shows Inbox, Archive, Spam and Trash. Labels stay in the Label menu. Moving an email out of Spam or Trash takes it out of there, and Trash asks before deleting, as the Delete button does.
- **No crash on an odd AI reply.** A reply from your AI provider with its braces in the wrong order now shows an error instead of quitting Shift.
