title: Accounts in every folder
---

- With several accounts, every folder opens into a row per account, with each account's unread count under Inbox. Click a folder again or its arrow to open or close it, and Shift remembers which are open.
- Each account has a small avatar with its initial, so accounts no longer look like labels of the same colour.
- The people on an email are now chips you can click: write to them, copy their address, find your emails with them, or reply to them alone.
- A draft in a conversation is clearly marked, with Edit and Delete. Deleting a draft, there or in Drafts, now removes only the draft and keeps the rest of the conversation.
- Archiving, deleting, moving and pinning say so in a new toast, with Undo and its shortcut. Quick moves in a row merge into one: "Archived 3 emails".
- Draft with AI reads each email's own words, so it no longer misses the latest email in a long thread.
- Pinned and moved emails glide to their new place in the list, and folders slide open and shut.
- Settings opens above the composer, the check-for-mail button sits in the title bar, and New Email shows ⌘N.
- Menus close when you click into an email, and a saved draft shows up in its conversation straight away.
