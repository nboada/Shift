title: Label colours and smarter auto-label
---

Fixes and small improvements to labels and AI.

- **Pick a colour when you create a label** with the + in the sidebar.
- **New labels show up straight away**, without restarting Shift, and an open label shows its name at the top of the list.
- **Auto-label also covers mail you've already read**, so opening an email from its notification no longer skips it.
- **Label my Inbox now**, in Settings → AI, applies your label rules to the emails already in your Inbox.
- **Write with AI and Draft a reply are quicker**, using medium effort with Claude.
