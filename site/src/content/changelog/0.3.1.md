title: Tidier composer
---

A calmer composer and a few small fixes.

- **Write with AI** sits at the top right of the message, easy to find.
- **⌘A selects what you wrote**, not your signature or the quoted email. Press it again to select everything.
- **Labels sit beside an email's subject**, without the "Labelled by AI" line.
- **Calmer buttons and dialogs.** Outlined secondary buttons, a softer focus ring and a clearer red for delete.
