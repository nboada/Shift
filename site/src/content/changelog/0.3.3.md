title: A composer you can move
---

- Drag the email window by its top bar, resize it from any edge, or dock it in the corner to read other mail while you write.
- While AI drafts or rewrites, the message area shows where the text is coming.
- Discard a closed draft straight from its "Draft saved" card.
- Follow-ups: when someone replies in a conversation you wrote in, it gets your "needs my reply" label even if it had no follow-up label yet.
- Accented letters now show properly in emails that mislabel their text encoding.
- Focus outlines are gone.
