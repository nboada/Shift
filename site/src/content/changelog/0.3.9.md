- Settings search finds settings by their heading, so "signature" finds every signature setting, including each account's own.
- In the wide list, the sender stands out most, then the subject, then the preview. The sender column is narrower, so subjects start closer to the names.
- The back arrow above an open email sits evenly between the edge and the buttons.
