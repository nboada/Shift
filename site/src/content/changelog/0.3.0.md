title: Add iCloud, Fastmail or your own domain
---

Shift now works with email accounts beyond Gmail.

- **Add any IMAP account.** Add email accounts from any provider that offers IMAP, such as iCloud, Fastmail, Yahoo or your own domain, with the email address and password. Shift finds the mail server for you.
- **Folders show as labels.** Applying a label moves the email into that folder. Read, pin, archive, trash, spam, drafts and sending all work, offline changes included.
- **Gmail works as before.** Every account can sit in one inbox.
