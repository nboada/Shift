title: Draft replies with AI in one click
---

Replying with AI is now one click away, and the open email keeps up with new mail.

- **Draft a reply with AI.** A new button at the right of the reader toolbar, and an item in the right-click menu, open a reply and draft it with AI straight away.
- **New messages show up.** A reply that arrives in the email you have open now appears without reopening it.
- **Smoother labels.** A label's colour changes at once, and a row's labels step aside when you hover its buttons.
