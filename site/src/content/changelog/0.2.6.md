title: A tidier AI menu
---

The AI menu in the composer now looks and works like the rest of Shift's menus.

- **Each option has an icon**, and the menu opens above the composer so it never covers what you're writing.
- **Rewrite options appear once there's text to rewrite.** An empty email shows only what you can use.
