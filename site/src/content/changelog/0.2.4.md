version: "0.2.4"
date: 2026-11-25
title: Safer sending, offline actions and older mail
---

A release focused on reliability: nothing you write or send should go missing, and Shift copes better with big mailboxes and flaky connections.

- **Undo send survives a quit or restart.** An email in its countdown still goes out, and Undo cancels it. Cancelling a scheduled email can no longer also send it, and a lost connection can't send the same email twice.
- **Drafts aren't lost.** If a draft can't be saved when you close it, it stays open, and quitting Shift saves the one you're writing first. Emails in Drafts now reopen in the composer.
- **Replies go to Reply-To** when a mailing list or help desk sets it.
- **Archiving and labelling work offline.** Changes wait and reach Gmail when you're back online, in order.
- **Older mail loads as you scroll**, in every mailbox, including Trash and Spam.
- **One deleted reply no longer hides its conversation** from the Inbox.
- **Safer email display.** Emails are cleaned before they're shown, and remote images stay blocked when they're off.
- **Keyboard menus.** Label, Move to and right-click menus work with the arrow keys.
- **Clearer bulk actions.** Mark all as read asks first, and actions across accounts say so.
- Smoother with large mailboxes, and gentler on Gmail's limits.
