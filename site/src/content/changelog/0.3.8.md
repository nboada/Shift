title: Updates when you want them
---

- **Updates are your choice.** When a new version is out, Shift shows what's new and asks before installing. Choose Later and a small icon next to Settings keeps it waiting. To install updates in the background as before, turn on Settings → General → Install updates automatically.
- Drag accounts in Settings → Accounts to change their order. The sidebar and the From menu follow it.
- Settings has a search field. Press ⌘F to find any setting and jump to it.
- Undo send can wait 3 seconds, now the default.
- Settings → Composer lists Replying and Sending first, with Signature last.
- Messages at the bottom of the window slide away when they close.
