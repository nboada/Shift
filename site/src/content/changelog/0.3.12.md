title: Safer by default
---

- Folder and label names show safely in menus, whatever characters they contain.
- A custom AI server must use an https:// address, unless it runs on your Mac or your local network, so your mail and API key never cross the internet unencrypted.
- Shift's data folder, which holds your cached mail, can only be opened by your own user account on the Mac.
