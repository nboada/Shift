title: Calendar invites
---

Invites now show up as events, and a few right-click menus got tidier.

- **Calendar invites.** An invite shows as an event card in the email, with the date, time and length, and you can answer Yes, Maybe or No without leaving Shift. Your answer goes to the organizer by email, so Shift doesn't need access to your calendar.
- **Tidier menus in Spam, Trash and Sent.** Right-click shows only what makes sense there: Not spam in Spam, Delete forever in Trash, and no Mark as unread or Spam in Sent.
