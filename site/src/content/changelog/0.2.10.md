title: Quick on big mailboxes
---

Shift stays fast with hundreds of emails in a list, and reordering the sidebar feels right.

- **No more lag in big mailboxes.** Opening, archiving, switching mailboxes and scrolling were slowed down by redrawing every email in the list each time. The list now draws what you can see and adds more as you scroll, so each change takes a few milliseconds instead of a few hundred.
- **Instant hover.** List rows highlight the moment the pointer reaches them.
- **Better sidebar reordering.** Drag a mailbox or label and it lifts and follows the pointer while the others slide out of the way. Letting go no longer opens it.
