title: Snappier reading
---

- Older emails in a conversation open straight away at their full height. Shift prepares them in the background while the conversation is open.
- A reply you send replaces its draft in the conversation as soon as it goes out, with no need to reload.
- Archiving, deleting or moving an email fades it out of the list while the rest close up.
- Email text is evened out in proportion, so small print such as signatures stays smaller than the body. Choose the size in Settings → Appearance → Emails.
- Settings → Accessibility has text sizes for the sidebar and the message list.
- Dropdowns in Settings, such as Zoom, open again.
