title: Clearer labels and menus
---

Labels are easier to read at a glance, and the menus that use them look like the rest of Shift.

- **See which labels an email has.** The Label menu shows each label's colour, with a check mark on the ones already applied. Move to shows label colours too, and the account switcher uses account colours and check marks instead of checkboxes.
- **Colour dots stay round.** They no longer stretch into bars in menus, including the label colour picker.
- **"Label my Inbox now" shows its progress.** In Settings → AI, the AI icon pulses while it sorts your Inbox, then shows whether it worked.
