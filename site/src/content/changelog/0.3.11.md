title: Tidier drafts
---

- A new email is kept as a draft only once you've written something: at least 10 characters, a recipient or a file. Switching the From account no longer saves a draft that's only your signature.
- Tab in the To field moves on to Subject (or Cc and Bcc when they're open), and turns what you typed into a recipient on the way.
- The Cc Bcc button closes the Cc and Bcc rows again, keeping any that have recipients.
