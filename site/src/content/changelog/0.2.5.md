title: Readable emails, safer email display and faster bulk actions
---

Fixes for the rest of our audit: emails that showed blank or garbled, a stronger wall around email content, and quicker bulk actions.

- **Emails that showed blank or garbled now display.** Long emails Gmail stores separately are fetched, and emails in other character sets (common outside English) are decoded properly.
- **Emails are fully walled off from Shift.** Each email now runs separately from the app, so nothing in an email can reach your mail or settings.
- **Bulk actions are faster.** Archiving, labelling and Clean up send one request per account instead of one per email, and you can select up to 250 emails at once.
- **Editing a scheduled email keeps it scheduled.** It pauses while you edit and goes out at the same time when you close it.
- **Undo after Delete puts mail back where it was**, not in the Inbox.
- **Fewer interruptions.** A sync error shows once, not every 30 seconds, and never covers Undo or Restart.
- **Signing out warns about scheduled emails** that won't be sent.
- **One damaged account no longer stops Shift opening.** The other accounts open, and Shift explains how to add it back.
- **Settings changes aren't lost** when you quit straight after making them.
- Sent emails no longer include your Mac's name in a hidden header, and AI writing help is harder to trick with text inside an email.
