The first build of Shift, for early access testers.

- **Several Gmail accounts in one inbox**, each with its own colour, or one account at a time.
- **Instant search** from a copy of your mail kept on your Mac.
- **Keyboard first**: archive, reply, label and move without the mouse, with a ⌘K command palette. Every shortcut can be remapped.
- **Rich composer** with formatting, signatures, attachments and drag and drop.
- **Send later** and **undo send**.
- **Notifications** for new mail, optionally only from people.
- **Remote images blocked** until you load them, so senders can't see when you open their email.
