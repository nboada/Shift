title: A tidier list and reader
---

The list and the open email are tidier, and AI labelling and Clean up are calmer.

- **Switch accounts from the list.** Coloured dots next to the list title show one account at a time; click again for all.
- **A tidier list.** Labels sit at the right end of each email's preview, pinned emails show a solid pin, and the fade at the bottom of the list is gone.
- **Subject stays in view.** The open email's subject is smaller, stays at the top while you scroll, and shows its labels beside it.
- **Calmer AI labelling.** Emails that already have one of your labels are left alone, and "Label my Inbox now" runs in the background with a corner card.
- **Cancel Clean up.** Stop a Clean up while it's sorting, from the sheet or its corner card.
